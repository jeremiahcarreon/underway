How to play

Each turn you may maneuver one ship (advance or reverse one cell along its heading, or rotate 90° about its stern — rotating ends your turn), then fire one shot at the enemy ocean.

A hit reveals the ship class, never the section or heading. Firing on a section that is already burning reports Confirmed: still here — it costs a shot but proves the ship has not moved. A ship whose stern is hit is crippled instantly. Enough hits also cripple: Patrol 1, Destroyer 2, Submarine 2, Battleship 2, Carrier 3. Any damaged ship can no longer rotate. Damage travels with the ship. Sink all five to win.
